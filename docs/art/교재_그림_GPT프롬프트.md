# 게임 속 교재 그림 프롬프트 (GPT 이미지 생성용)

1층 책방 안락의자에서 읽는 **게임 속 교재(9장)**의 그림입니다.
어린이천문대 교재 12장 「우주 속의 지구」(168~179쪽) 내용을 바탕으로 했습니다.

## 쓰는 방법

- 장마다 **"공통 규칙" + 그 장의 프롬프트**를 함께 붙여 넣으세요.
- 그림 속 글자는 게임이 따로 씁니다. 그래서 **그림에는 글자를 넣지 않습니다.** 생성형 AI는 한글을 자주 틀리게 씁니다.
- 교재 PDF를 첨부할 때는 **"구도와 내용만 참고하고, 교재의 캐릭터(주황 캐릭터, 웃는 해·지구 캐릭터 등)는 그리지 말 것"**을 꼭 적어 주세요. 교재 캐릭터는 교재의 저작물입니다.

## 공통 규칙 (모든 장에 붙여 넣기)

```
Children's astronomy workbook illustration for 4th graders (age 10).
Style: clean flat vector illustration, soft pastel colors on a light cream paper background,
thick friendly outlines, simple shapes, scientifically accurate.
Landscape 3:2 (1536 x 1024). Leave the top 15% empty (plain background) for a title.
NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO labels anywhere in the image.
NO cartoon faces on the Sun, Earth or Moon. No copyrighted characters.
```

## 1. 지구의 자전과 낮과 밤

```
The Earth seen from above the North Pole, a large circle in the center, with the Sun on the far left
shining parallel light rays. The half of the Earth facing the Sun is bright (day), the other half dark blue (night).
A curved arrow around the Earth shows counterclockwise rotation (seen from above the North Pole).
A small child figure stands on the boundary between day and night (evening side).
On the right, a small inset of a Foucault pendulum seen from above: a round floor dial with tick marks,
a dotted line for the swing direction staying fixed while a curved arrow shows the floor turning.
```

## 2. 별의 일주운동

```
A long-exposure night photograph style illustration of the northern sky over a small island observatory with a dome.
Concentric circular star trails around one bright point near the top center (Polaris).

Dark silhouette of a hill and the sea at the bottom.
```

## 3. 북극성 찾기

```
Northern night sky diagram on navy background: the Big Dipper on the left, Cassiopeia (W shape) on the right,
the Little Dipper in the middle ending at Polaris.
Dotted guide line from the two pointer stars at the end of the Big Dipper's bowl, extended five equal steps
(small arcs) to Polaris. A second dotted guide line from Cassiopeia's W toward Polaris, also five equal steps.
Stars as simple glowing dots with thin constellation lines.
```

## 4. 지구는 둥글다 ① 수평선 너머의 배

```
Three small panels side by side showing the same passenger ferry moving away over the sea
across a curved ocean surface (the curve of the Earth slightly exaggerated):
near (the whole ship visible), farther (the lower hull hidden below the horizon), farthest (only the mast top light visible).
```

## 5. 지구는 둥글다 ② 월식의 그림자

```
Left: a diagram of the Sun, the Earth and the Moon in a straight line; the Earth casts a cone-shaped dark shadow
and the Moon is entering the shadow.
Right: a sequence of five full moons arranged in a gentle diagonal arc across a black sky,
progressively covered by a round reddish-brown shadow, the middle one fully copper red (total lunar eclipse),
the shadow edge on each moon clearly a curved arc.
```

## 6. 지구는 둥글다 ③ 남쪽 하늘의 별

```
A bright star (Canopus) shines low just above the horizon for the southern observer,
right at the horizon for the middle observer, and hidden below the horizon for the northern observer
```

## 7. 계절은 왜 생길까?

```
The Earth's orbit around the Sun seen at an angle (an ellipse-shaped ring), the Sun in the center.
The Earth drawn at two opposite positions: June (left) and December (right).
The Earth's axis is tilted the same direction (to the right) at both positions:
in June the northern half leans toward the Sun, in December away from it.
Below, two small inset panels: a flashlight shining straight down making a small bright circle (summer),
and shining at a low angle making a wide dim oval (winter).
```

## 8. 월식은 어떻게 진행될까?

```
A row of seven moon phases of a total lunar eclipse from left to right on a black background:
full moon, partial (a round dark bite on the left edge), larger partial, total (deep copper red),
partial ending, smaller partial, full moon again.
Under the row, a thin timeline bar with small tick marks (no numbers).
```
