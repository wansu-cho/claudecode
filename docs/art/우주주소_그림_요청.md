# 우주 주소 그림 7장 요청서 (진엔딩)

진엔딩에서 "우주 주소"가 지구부터 관측 가능한 우주까지 일곱 단계로 넓어지는 장면의 그림입니다.
지금은 코드로 그린 단순한 그림(동그라미와 점)이 들어가 있고, 그림이 오면 단계별로 바로 바꿉니다.

- 생성형 AI: 제미나이 또는 GPT 모두 괜찮습니다. **7장 모두 같은 AI, 같은 대화창**에서 만들면 그림체가 잘 맞습니다
- 첨부: **별지기의 방 그림**(그림체와 색감 참고)
- 첫 장을 받은 뒤, **그 그림을 첨부해서** 나머지 장을 요청하면 더 잘 맞습니다

## 공통 규칙 (모든 장에 붙여 넣기)

```
Pixel art space illustration for a children's astronomy game, same style as the attached image
(dark outlines, 2-3 shade levels, rich deep navy and purple space background, warm gold accents).
Size 1920 x 1080 (16:9). The bottom 25% of the image must be plain dark space (a caption box goes there).
Keep the main subject in the upper-middle area.
NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO labels, NO arrows. No people.
Scientifically accurate shapes (this is for a science lesson for 10-year-olds).
```

---

## 1. 지구

```
The Earth seen from space, large in the center: blue oceans, green and brown continents with East Asia
(the Korean peninsula) facing the viewer, white swirling clouds, a thin glowing atmosphere edge.
Sunlight comes from the left: the left half is day, the right half is night with tiny city lights.
```

## 2. 태양계

```
The Solar System seen from above at a slight angle: the Sun glowing in the center and eight planets
on thin elliptical orbit lines, in the correct order (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn with rings,
Uranus, Neptune). Earth is the third planet, slightly highlighted with a soft gold ring around it.
Planet sizes may be enlarged so they are visible, but keep their relative order and colors correct.
```

## 3. 우리은하

```
The Milky Way galaxy seen face-on: a barred spiral galaxy with a bright golden central bar and
two main spiral arms plus smaller arms, blue-white star clouds and dark dust lanes.
A tiny glowing gold marker (a small circle) about two-thirds of the way out from the center on a minor arm
shows where the Sun is.
```

## 4. 국부은하군

```
The Local Group of galaxies: two large spiral galaxies (the Milky Way on the left, the larger Andromeda galaxy
on the right, tilted), the smaller Triangulum spiral galaxy, and a few dozen small dwarf galaxies
as faint fuzzy blobs scattered around them. The Milky Way has a tiny gold marker.
```

## 5. 처녀자리 초은하단

```
The Virgo Supercluster: hundreds of tiny galaxies (small spirals and ellipticals as glowing specks)
gathered into groups and clusters, with one big dense cluster (the Virgo Cluster) near the center.
Our Local Group is a tiny group near the edge, marked with a small gold circle.
```

## 6. 라니아케아 초은하단

```
The Laniakea Supercluster: a vast web of galaxies drawn as countless tiny points of light,
arranged in flowing streams like rivers that all curve toward one central region (the Great Attractor),
like branches of a tree or veins of a leaf. Soft blue and gold colors.
A tiny gold marker on the edge shows where we are.
```

## 7. 관측 가능한 우주

```
The observable universe as a giant sphere: inside it, the cosmic web of filaments and knots of galaxies
like a glowing sponge or spider web, brighter knots where clusters meet, dark empty voids between them.
The outer edge of the sphere glows faintly (the farthest light we can see).
A tiny gold point in the exact center marks us.
```
